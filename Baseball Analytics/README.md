Problems that are being Solved.

1. Pitch Outcome — split into three honestly-scoped sub-models
Original question: predict ball / called strike / foul / whiff / hit-in-play in one
5-class model.

2. Exit Velocity Regression
Question: For fastballs put in play, how much of the resulting exit velocity can be
explained by the pitch itself (vs. the swing)?

4. Team Fastball Velocity — Trend & Forecasting (time series)
Question: how does the Cubs' team-average fastball velocity trend over time, and
can it be forecast a few weeks out?

5. Pitcher Fastball Archetype Clustering (unsupervised)
Question: Do Cubs fastball pitchers group into distinct "shapes" based on velocity,
spin rate, movement, release point, and extension — independent of outcomes?
